About the Mini Goggle

The Tidepop Mini Goggle was designed from the ground up for little faces — not as a scaled-down adult goggle, but purpose-built for ages 3 to 7. The compact frame sits comfortably without sliding, and the ultra-soft silicone seal seals gently without the pressure marks that put young kids off swimming.

The easy-adjust ratchet strap can be tightened or loosened in seconds — important for swim lesson mornings when there's no time to fumble. Comes in four bold two-tone colour combinations that kids actually get excited about.
