About the Mask Goggle

The Tidepop Mask Goggle delivers an unrivalled panoramic field of vision. The wide-frame design wraps around the eyes, removing the tunnel-vision effect of traditional goggles and giving swimmers a clear, open view of the underwater world.

Perfect for ocean swims, snorkelling, open water events, and recreational swimming. The soft silicone seal conforms to the face without leaving pressure marks, and the single-adjustment strap stays put through the longest sessions.
