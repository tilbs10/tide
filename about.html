Our Story

The water should feel like home.

We started Tidepop with one simple belief — that a child who feels comfortable and confident in the water will carry that love with them for life.

It started at the pool edge.

Every swimming lesson, the same scene played out — kids fussing with leaking goggles, uncomfortable seals leaving red marks, straps that wouldn't stay. Small frustrations that added up to one big problem: children who were distracted, uncomfortable, and slowly losing their enthusiasm for the water.

We knew it didn't have to be that way. The right pair of goggles should disappear the moment you put them on — no leaks, no pressure, no fidgeting. Just clear water and open sky.

So we built Tidepop. Goggles that fit properly, feel great, and come in colours that kids actually get excited about. Because when the gear gets out of the way, the joy rushes in.

More than goggles.

We're building something bigger than a product. Every child who grows up confident in the water goes on to discover something new — surfing, snorkelling, open water swimming, water polo, sailing. A whole life lived close to the ocean. That starts here, at the pool edge, at age five, with a pair of goggles that fits.
