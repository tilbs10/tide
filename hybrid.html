About the Hybrid Goggle

The Tidepop Hybrid Goggle is our flagship model — designed for swimmers who want the best of both worlds. A flexible silicone frame delivers a leak-free seal, while the anti-fog UV400 lenses give crisp, clear vision from the first lap to the last.

The dual-adjustment quick-release strap fits a wide range of head sizes, and three interchangeable nose bridge sizes ensure a custom-level fit out of the box. Suitable for pool and open water.
